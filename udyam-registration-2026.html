Udyam Registration 2026: Free MSME Registration Step by Step

How to register an MSME on the official free Udyam portal in 2026, including eligibility, Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN and the registration certificate.

Udyam Registration is the Government of India’s official online process for recognising eligible micro, small and medium enterprises. It is free, paperless and based on self-declaration. This guide explains what to check before starting and how to avoid misleading paid registration websites.

What is Udyam Registration?

Udyam is the official MSME registration system administered by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. A registered enterprise receives a permanent Udyam Registration Number and an online certificate with a QR code. The certificate does not require periodic renewal. Registration does not itself guarantee a loan, subsidy or government contract.

MSME classification applicable in 2026

Revised classification limits have applied since 1 April 2025. Both the investment and turnover thresholds are relevant.

CategoryInvestment in plant/machinery or equipmentAnnual turnover
MicroNot exceeding ₹2.5 croreNot exceeding ₹10 crore
SmallNot exceeding ₹25 croreNot exceeding ₹100 crore
MediumNot exceeding ₹125 croreNot exceeding ₹500 crore

Classification is based on the applicable official rules, including linked PAN/GST data and aggregation requirements. Export turnover is treated according to the notified rules.

Before you begin

  • Have the relevant Aadhaar number and access to its linked mobile for OTP verification.
  • Keep the enterprise PAN and GSTIN available where applicable under the rules.
  • Know the organisation type, business address, bank details and principal business activities/NIC codes.
  • Use only the official government website. The registration itself is free.

For a proprietorship, Aadhaar is generally that of the proprietor; for a partnership, the managing partner; and for an HUF, the karta. Companies and other specified entities provide the organisation’s PAN/GSTIN and the authorised signatory’s Aadhaar, as applicable.

Step-by-step registration process

  1. Visit the official Udyam Registration portal.
  2. Select the option for new entrepreneurs who have not registered as MSMEs.
  3. Enter the required Aadhaar details, give consent and complete OTP verification.
  4. Provide organisation type and PAN details; complete verification where prompted.
  5. Fill in the remaining enterprise information, including address, activities and other requested particulars.
  6. Review the declaration carefully, submit and complete any final OTP step.
  7. Retain the registration number and download/print the certificate from the official portal when available.

The portal may adapt its screens depending on entity type and tax registrations. Follow the instructions shown there rather than relying on an old screenshot.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Paying a private intermediary who presents their site as the government registration portal.
  • Using a different name or entity PAN from official records.
  • Selecting incorrect business activities or organisation type.
  • Creating multiple Udyam registrations for the same enterprise instead of adding activities under the existing registration.
  • Assuming Udyam automatically makes a business eligible for every government scheme.

Frequently asked questions

Is Udyam Registration free?

Yes. The official government portal does not charge a registration fee.

Does the Udyam certificate expire?

The official portal states that registration does not require renewal. Enterprise information may still need updating when relevant.

Is GSTIN mandatory for every applicant?

GSTIN requirements depend on applicability under GST law and the Udyam rules. Follow the official portal instructions for your enterprise type.

Does registration ensure bank finance?

No. Banks independently assess eligibility, repayment capacity, credit history and documentation.
