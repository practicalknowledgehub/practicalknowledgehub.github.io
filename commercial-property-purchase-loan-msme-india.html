Commercial Property Purchase Loan for MSMEs in India: SBI First, Bank Options and Eligibility

Compare property purchase finance with loans against existing property, and understand margin, valuation, repayment and bank documentation.

Updated October 2026 · Independent educational information

Buying a shop, office, warehouse or industrial unit can help a growing enterprise secure a long-term business location. Financing that purchase, however, is different from borrowing against a property you already own. Before applying, identify the property’s intended use, the borrower, the expected source of repayment and the lender’s acceptable security.

Commercial property purchase loan versus loan against property

A purchase or construction loan finances acquisition or development of a commercial asset. A loan against property (LAP) releases funds by mortgaging an existing eligible property, often for business expenditure. Lease rental discounting is another distinct product, linked to rental cash flows. Do not assume that every LAP product permits purchasing a new commercial property.

Banks to explore: SBI first

1. State Bank of India (SBI): Asset Backed Loan – Commercial Real Estate

SBI publishes a dedicated scheme for purchase or construction of offices, retail space, warehouses and other eligible commercial real estate. Its official description lists a borrower contribution of 25%, LTV of 50% of the property's realisable value, a drop-line overdraft structure and repayment periods of 12–72 months including moratorium. These are separate tests; 25% contribution does not mean automatic finance of 75% of the purchase price. Actual sanction depends on the bank's appraisal, security and current policy.

Read SBI’s official Commercial Real Estate scheme.

2. ICICI Bank: Commercial Property Purchase Loan

ICICI Bank lists commercial property purchase or construction finance among its loan offerings. The bank's general product pages refer to financing up to 75%, subject to conditions; the precise eligibility and borrower category must be checked directly. ICICI also has a separate NRI/OCI commercial property purchase product, which should not be confused with a standard resident-MSME facility.

ICICI Bank loan product overview · NRI-specific commercial property loan.

3. Other banks: confirm the exact end-use

Ask public-sector and private-sector banks whether they have an active commercial premises purchase/construction facility for your business constitution. Compare the actual sanction terms, not only advertisements for loan against property. Product availability can differ by branch, property type and location.

Worked illustration: how margin and LTV differ

Suppose a commercial premises costs ₹1.00 crore and a bank determines its realisable value at ₹90 lakh. A 25% borrower contribution test would imply ₹25 lakh from the borrower. But a 50% LTV against ₹90 lakh limits finance to ₹45 lakh, before other eligibility limits. The borrower may therefore need to arrange ₹55 lakh plus taxes and transaction costs. Illustration only; actual valuation and policy may differ.

What the lender typically examines

  • Business constitution, Udyam registration where applicable, PAN/KYC and banking conduct
  • IT returns, audited financial statements, GST returns, projected cash flows and existing obligations
  • Sale agreement, title chain, encumbrance position, approvals, building plan and permitted commercial use
  • Independent valuation, marketability, legal due diligence, own contribution and repayment capacity
  • Whether rent, business profits or a combination will service the debt

Compare the total borrowing cost

Ask for interest benchmark and spread, reset frequency, processing fees, valuation/legal expenses, mortgage charges, insurance and foreclosure conditions. A lower advertised rate may not mean lower overall cost.

Frequently asked questions

Can an MSME borrow to buy commercial land alone?

Some facilities may finance eligible land and building, while others require a completed or approved commercial asset. Obtain written end-use confirmation from the lender.

Is SBI ABL Saral the same as SBI commercial real estate finance?

No. ABL Saral is a different asset-backed business finance offering. Check the purpose and conditions of the specific scheme.

Does property value alone decide the loan amount?

No. LTV, acceptable security, repayment capacity, borrower contribution, credit history and bank policy all matter.
