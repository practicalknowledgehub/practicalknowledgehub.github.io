Loan Repayment Capacity for MSMEs: DSCR and Cash Flow

Understand cash available for debt service, DSCR and realistic stress testing before taking a business loan.

Updated October 2026 · Independent educational information

A business should assess whether it can service a proposed term loan after operating expenses, tax, maintenance investment and existing commitments. Profit alone is not the same as available cash.

Debt service coverage ratio

For a simplified illustrative check, DSCR = cash available for debt service ÷ (scheduled principal + interest). Lenders may define the numerator and treatment of tax, depreciation and existing debt differently.

Example

Suppose cash available for debt service is ₹18 lakh annually and scheduled principal plus interest is ₹12 lakh. The illustrative DSCR is 1.50. If available cash falls to ₹12 lakh, DSCR becomes 1.00 and the buffer disappears.

Assess repayment under stress

  • Test lower turnover and margins, delayed collections and increased raw-material costs.
  • Include all existing term-loan instalments and relevant interest.
  • Separate temporary working-capital requirements from long-term debt capacity.
  • Compare annual DSCR with the weakest month or quarter for seasonal operations.
