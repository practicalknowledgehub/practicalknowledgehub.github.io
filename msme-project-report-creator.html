How to use this first version

  1. Enter genuine business details or select Load fictional sample to understand the workflow.
  2. Fill the project cost and funding sources in ₹ lakh, ensuring they balance.
  3. Enter sales, direct costs, operating expenses, borrowing terms and cash-credit assumptions.
  4. Choose Generate report preview and review the five-year projections and DSCR calculations.
  5. Use Print / Save as PDF to produce an A4 document, or download a standalone HTML report.

What is included?

The report includes an applicant profile, narrative sections, project cost, means of finance, assumed borrowing structure, annual profit projections, a term-loan repayment summary, an illustrative DSCR and a simple Year 1 break-even estimate. Figures are estimates based only on the entered information.

What should be added before a bank application?

Bank loan project reports normally need evidence supporting estimates: quotations, site and ownership papers, expected capacity utilisation, market assumptions, promoter contribution, implementation schedule, licences, working capital assessment and other documents suitable for the chosen loan.
