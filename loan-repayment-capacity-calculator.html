How to use and interpret this calculator

Use the calculator above as a starting point for planning. The notes below explain the assumptions and how to review the result in an Indian MSME financing context.

Cash available for debt service

Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) generally compares cash available for scheduled debt service with principal plus interest due. Exact numerator adjustments differ by lender and assessment methodology.

Worked business example

Suppose sustainable annual cash available for debt service is ₹36 lakh and scheduled annual principal plus interest is ₹30 lakh. The indicative DSCR is 1.20. This means projected cash coverage exceeds scheduled service by ₹6 lakh before unmodelled shocks.

Checks before relying on the result

Check whether depreciation, interest, tax, drawings, existing obligations and working-capital movements are treated consistently. Compare projections with actual cash generation and repayment schedules.

What the result means for an MSME

A positive ratio does not guarantee loan approval. Banks also examine promoter contribution, collateral where applicable, repayment history, industry risk and the credibility of projected sales.

Practical review checklist

  • Keep input figures and their period or units consistent.
  • Recalculate using a conservative scenario rather than relying only on an optimistic forecast.
  • Reconcile the assumptions with recent accounts, invoices, bank statements and contractual terms.
  • Discuss product-specific eligibility and methodology with the lender before making a financing decision.
