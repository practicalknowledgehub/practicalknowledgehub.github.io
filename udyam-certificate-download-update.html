Udyam Certificate Download and Update: Official Steps

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Find the official Udyam portal, retrieve your registration certificate, and understand how to update business information safely.

This educational guide explains key considerations for Indian entrepreneurs and small businesses. Confirm current scheme rules and individual lender requirements before making decisions.

Use the official portal only

Udyam registration and its official services are available through the Government of India portal. Avoid third-party websites that imitate government pages or demand unnecessary registration fees.

How to download or print the certificate

Visit the official Udyam Registration portal. Choose the option to print or verify the Udyam Registration Certificate, provide the requested registration details and complete the portal’s authentication steps. The precise on-screen labels and verification options may change.

How to update details

Use the official update facility linked from the Udyam portal. Keep PAN, GST and other applicable enterprise information consistent with official records. Some fields may be system-linked and may not be editable directly.

What if the number is lost?

Look for the official forgot-registration-number or retrieval option on the Udyam portal and follow the available identity verification process. Do not share OTPs with an intermediary.

Why correct information matters

Banks, customers and government departments may use Udyam details for verification. Mismatches in business name, constitution or classification can delay documentation, so confirm details before submitting a loan application.

Registration is not loan approval

An Udyam certificate establishes registration details under the MSME framework; it does not itself guarantee bank credit, a subsidy or a government contract.
