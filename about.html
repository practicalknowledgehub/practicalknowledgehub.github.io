About our website

About MSME Knowledge Hub

MSME Knowledge Hub is an independent educational website created to make business finance and banking concepts easier to understand for Indian entrepreneurs, students and finance professionals.

What we publish

Our articles explain topics such as MSME business loans, credit appraisal, working capital assessment, financial ratios, cash flow and business planning. We aim to use straightforward language, practical examples and links to official sources where relevant.

Our approach

  • Explain financial concepts step by step, including worked numerical examples.
  • Clearly label illustrative figures and distinguish them from bank-specific policies.
  • Review content for clarity and update it when important information changes.
  • Encourage readers to verify current requirements with their lender or relevant regulator.

Our featured book

We also share information about A Practical Guide for Indian MSMEs by Madhumitha V, a practical resource on MSME finance and credit appraisal. The book is presented separately from our free educational articles.

Independence and limitations

MSME Knowledge Hub is not an official website of the Reserve Bank of India, SIDBI, any bank or any government agency. Articles are for general educational purposes and are not personalized lending, investment, tax or legal advice. Lending criteria and regulatory requirements can change.
