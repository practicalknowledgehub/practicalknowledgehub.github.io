Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

MSME Knowledge Hub (the “Website”) provides free educational content about MSME finance, banking and financial analysis. This policy describes how information may be handled when you visit our website.

1. Information you provide

At present, the Website does not provide user registration, an account system, or a contact form. We do not directly ask visitors to submit personal information through the Website. Please avoid sharing confidential financial or account details through public channels.

2. Website hosting and technical information

The Website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider and the services used to deliver the Website may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, request timestamps and server logs according to their own policies. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies and analytics

We do not currently operate our own advertising or analytics scripts on these pages. Third-party services involved in hosting or links you follow may have their own cookie and data practices. If we introduce analytics, advertising or other tracking technologies, this policy will be updated to explain them and any applicable consent choices.

4. External websites

Our pages may link to book retailers, government bodies, banking institutions and other websites. We do not control their content, privacy practices or cookies. Please review their respective privacy notices before sharing information.

5. Advertising and affiliate links

At the time of this update, the Website does not display Google AdSense advertising. If advertisements or affiliate arrangements are introduced, we will update this notice with relevant disclosures, including any data collection and cookie information required by applicable law and advertising partners.

6. Children’s privacy

The Website is intended as general business and financial education content. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as the Website grows or its services change. The revised date will be shown at the top of this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, please check our Contact page. A dedicated contact email address will be published there when available.
