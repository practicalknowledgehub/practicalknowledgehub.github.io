Negotiated repayment methodology

Specify the principal repayable in each year, based on projected cash flows. Each annual principal amount is distributed evenly over 12 months; monthly interest is calculated on the reducing opening balance. The annual principal allocations must equal the original loan amount and the final closing principal must be zero.

Rounding differences are adjusted in each year's final monthly principal instalment. No processing fees, GST, moratorium, prepayments or rate changes are included. Banks may use daily-product interest or other sanction-specific conventions.
