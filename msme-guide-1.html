Frequently asked questions

What should an MSME prepare before applying for a bank loan?

Common requirements include business and identity documents, financial statements, bank account information, details of the proposed borrowing, and financial projections where applicable. Requirements vary by lender and loan product.

What is credit appraisal?

Credit appraisal is the lender's evaluation of a borrower's financial position, business prospects, repayment capacity and other risks before deciding on a loan.

What is the difference between a term loan and working capital finance?

A term loan typically finances longer-term business assets or investments. Working capital finance helps fund day-to-day operating requirements, such as inventory and receivables.
